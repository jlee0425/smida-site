Privacy

Effective and last updated 22 October 2026 · Developer: LEE JEONGKYU

Smida is a training log with no account and no server. What you log stays on your phone. This policy explains what the app keeps there, the one thing that leaves it on its own, and how you stay in control.

Who we are

Smida is made by LEE JEONGKYU, an individual developer, who is responsible for your personal information under this policy (the controller under EU and UK law). Contact: hello@smida.fit. A postal address is given without delay on request.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • Training data you enter: workouts, sessions, scores, loads, notes, PRs, lift goals and benchmark attempts.
  • Settings: units, language, weekly goal, training focus, your max heart rate if you enter it, and the division (men or women) you pick for races and Rx loads.
  • Body weight (optional): weights you enter, and weights imported from Health Connect if you allow it.
  • Health Connect data (Android, optional): heart rate, exercise sessions, active calories, steps and weight, read only if you allow it. See "Health Connect" below.
  • Whiteboard scans (optional): the photo you take or pick is read on your phone and not saved. If you save a scan to the Inbox, the text read from it waits there until you log it or it expires (after 2 weeks unless you change this in Settings).
  • A tester code: if you enter one, it is checked on your phone and kept there.

How it's used

Only to run the app for you on your phone: to show your log, work out your PRs and progress, add watch stats to your sessions, show your weight trend, read workouts from whiteboard photos, and make share cards and exports when you ask. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

Under EU and UK law, our legal basis for the ML Kit metrics below and for handling email you send us is legitimate interest: keeping the scan feature working and secure, and answering you.

Health Connect

If you allow it, Smida reads heart rate, exercise sessions, active calories, steps and weight from Health Connect. Access is read-only: Smida never writes to Health Connect. It reads only while you use the app, for example when you log or open a session, link it to a watch workout or refresh its stats, open Progress, or import your weight.

For a linked session, Smida keeps the average and maximum heart rate with a heart-rate graph, steps, active calories, the watch workout's start and end times and title, and the app and device that recorded it. For weight, it keeps each reading's value and time.

This data is used only to show these stats in Smida. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred to anyone by Smida, and never used to decide credit, insurance or employment. It stays on your phone unless you export a backup yourself, which includes the stats saved with your sessions. The developer has no access to it. Smida's use of it follows the Health Connect permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can turn off any of these permissions at any time in Health Connect's app permissions. Stats already saved are kept with their session on your phone; Erase all data or uninstalling removes them (deleting a session only hides them, see below).

What leaves your phone

Smida has no server and sends nothing to the developer. One thing leaves your phone without you sending it:

  • Whiteboard scanning uses Google ML Kit on your phone. The photo and the text read from it never leave your phone. ML Kit sends Google technical metrics about the scan feature, such as device model and OS, app version, an app-installation identifier, speed, image format and size, and error codes. Google uses them to maintain the feature and detect misuse. Scan is optional: before your first scan the app tells you about these metrics and lets you choose not to use it, and you can turn it off in Settings → Movements & scanning → Scan. If you never use Scan, nothing is sent to Google; once you switch it off, Scan sends nothing new. See the ML Kit terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Everything else leaves only when you choose:

  • How-to videos open on YouTube, where YouTube's terms and the Google Privacy Policy apply.
  • Send feedback opens your own mail app with a draft that includes the app version, build, OS version, phone model and language. Nothing is sent until you send it.
  • Backups, CSV files and share cards are made on your phone and leave it only when you save or share them.

Email you send us

The developer receives personal information only if you write to hello@smida.fit, for example with a question, feedback or to join the beta.

  • What: your email address, your name if your mail shows it, the message and anything you attach. A feedback draft also carries the app version, build, OS version, phone model and language.
  • Why: only to answer you and, if you asked to join the beta, to send beta invites.
  • How long: until your question is settled, or until the beta ends if you joined it, and never more than 3 years. Then it is deleted from the mailbox and its trash. Ask and we will delete it sooner.
  • Who handles it: our domain host, Porkbun LLC (United States), forwards it to the developer's Gmail mailbox, which Google LLC (United States) stores.
  • How to refuse: don't write to us: the app works fully without it. If you already have, ask us to delete your mail.

What we don't do

  • No account and no sign-up: the developer has no copy of your data.
  • No analytics or advertising of our own, and no crash-reporting service.
  • No cookies, no tracking and no use of your advertising ID.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Backups and CSV files

A backup is a file you export yourself in Settings → Data & Backup. It is a plain JSON file and is not encrypted: anyone who has the file can read it. It holds your whole log, including deleted items, your body weights, your settings and the Health Connect stats saved with your sessions.

A CSV export holds your sessions, their movements and your PR Book, for a spreadsheet. Deleted items are left out. It is plain text too.

You choose where each file goes: a folder on your phone, or any app in the share sheet, such as a cloud drive or email. Smida has no cloud storage of its own. Keep these files somewhere private, and send them only to people you'd show your health data to.

Security

Your data is protected by staying on your phone. It lives in Smida's private storage, which Android's app sandbox keeps out of reach of other apps. It is also left out of Android's cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer. The ML Kit metrics travel over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Use a screen lock: it protects Smida's data along with everything else on your phone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. When you delete a session or another item, it disappears from the app, but the record stays hidden in the app's database, and in any backup you export, until you erase all data or uninstall the app.

To delete everything, go to Settings → Data & Backup → Erase all data. It permanently deletes everything Smida stored on your phone, a tester code included. Uninstalling Smida does the same.

Neither touches what lives outside Smida: files you exported (backups, CSV files, share cards) stay where you saved them until you delete them, and Health Connect keeps its own data and the permission you gave Smida until you turn it off there.

Email you send us is kept as described under "Email you send us". Google keeps the ML Kit metrics under its own policy; Smida can't see or delete them.

Transfers outside your country

Smida itself transfers nothing abroad. Two things can reach companies in other countries. First, the ML Kit metrics:

  • Recipient: Google LLC (contact through the Google Privacy Policy).
  • Country: the United States, and other countries where Google runs servers.
  • What: the metrics listed under "What leaves your phone".
  • When and how: each time you use whiteboard scanning, over an encrypted connection.
  • Why and for how long: for Google to maintain ML Kit and detect misuse, kept under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • How to refuse: choose not to use Scan when the app asks, or turn it off in Settings → Movements & scanning → Scan. You can type any workout instead, and nothing else in the app sends data.

Second, email you send us:

  • Recipients: Porkbun LLC, which forwards it, and Google LLC (Gmail), which stores it.
  • Country: the United States, and other countries where Google runs servers.
  • What: the items listed under "Email you send us".
  • When and how: only when you send an email, through your own mail app.
  • Why and for how long: to answer you; kept as described under "Email you send us".
  • How to refuse: don't write to us. Nothing in the app needs email.

For the EU and UK: Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension. Email reaches other countries because you send it to us; that transfer is necessary to answer your request (GDPR Article 49(1)(b)).

For Japan: the United States has no comprehensive federal privacy law like Japan's APPI, though some states have their own; Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission publishes a summary. Google describes the protections it applies in its Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Because your data is on your phone, you can see, correct, export and delete it yourself at any time: edit or delete any entry, export a backup or CSV, or erase all data. For anything we hold, such as an email you sent, write to hello@smida.fit. We confirm it is you by replying to the address the request came from; a representative must send your signed authorisation. We reply within 10 days, free of charge.

  • EU, EEA and UK: you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
  • Japan: you can ask us to disclose, correct, stop using or delete personal information we hold, and to disclose records of providing it to others. You can also contact the Personal Information Protection Commission.
  • Korea: you can ask to view, correct or delete your personal information or to stop its processing, yourself or through a representative. For help with a privacy violation, contact the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972, kopico.go.kr) or the KISA Privacy Infringement Report Center (118, privacy.kisa.or.kr).

Children

Smida isn't directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has emailed us, tell us and we'll delete it.
